❓ Common Questions & Answers

Click any question to view
1. Why am I only allowed to change my username one time? +
To maintain identity consistency across your academic projects and prevent accidental renaming, our policy allows you to update your registered name exactly once. After the first change, your name is permanently locked.
2. How does login and registration work on GitHub Pages without PHP? +
GitHub Pages only hosts static files. We configured your system to use the browser's localStorage database engine. It stores your account, verifies your credentials, and maintains your active session across pages without needing a server.
3. Can I change my profile picture multiple times? +
Yes! Unlike your username, you can change your profile picture as many times as you like. You can upload any personal photo from your computer or switch between the built-in avatar presets (Coder, Student, Astronaut, etc.) anytime on your Profile page.
4. How do I log out or switch to another user? +
Click the red Logout button in your dashboard or profile navigation. This clears your active session and returns you to the login screen, where you can log in as another user or register a new account.
